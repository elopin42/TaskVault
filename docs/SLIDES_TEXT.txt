# TaskVault
Fixed prices. Earned trust. Guaranteed payment.

A company splits its project into fixed-price tasks. Each task's money is locked on Stellar before the work starts, and released to the freelancer's wallet the moment the company approves.

Hack Meridian · Track Composed · Freighter + USDC + Trustless Work

---

# The problem
- Freelancers get paid late, sometimes never.
- Small companies splitting a project between several freelancers juggle quotes, invoices and trust, with no shared track record.
- Big platforms typically take 10 to 20% and hold the money until their payout cycle.
- Price races to the bottom reward rushed work, not good work.

---

# The solution
- The company splits a project into tasks with a fixed price in USDC. No haggling.
- Freelancers apply with a message and their skills.
- A transparent score recommends a candidate. The company always chooses.
- Each task's money is locked in its own escrow before work starts, and released in about 5 seconds when the company approves the delivery.

---

# How it works (demo)
1. Connect the Freighter wallet: a signed message, no account, no password.
2. Create the project and its fixed-price tasks.
3. Freelancers apply; candidates are ranked live, "Recommended" on top.
4. Choose a freelancer, then "Fund the project": the price is locked in an escrow on Stellar.
5. The freelancer delivers a link, recorded on chain as evidence.
6. The company rates 1 to 5 stars and releases: USDC lands in the freelancer's wallet in seconds.

# Trust you can compute
score = 40 × already paid by this company
      + 8 × average rating from this company
      + 20 × share of the task's skills matched
Maximum 100. No AI, no black box.

Next task: Yanis already delivered 5/5 → 90 points, "Validated" + "Recommended". Nora, a newcomer with matching skills → 20 points. The company still decides; trust is earned task by task.

# Why Stellar: 3 building blocks, 1 flow
- Freighter: the wallet is the identity and the signature. TaskVault never holds a private key.
- USDC on Stellar: dollar-stable, settled in about 5 seconds for a fraction of a cent.
- Trustless Work on Soroban: one escrow per task. Nobody can run away with the money, not even the platform.
