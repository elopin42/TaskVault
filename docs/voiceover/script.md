# TaskVault — video voice-over (Polly, English US)

One `.ssml` file per scene = one audio clip in HyperFrames. `full.ssml` is the whole thing in one take.
Paste a file into Polly with the SSML toggle on, Download → `<scene>.mp3`.

## 01-hook — The problem

Freelancers get paid late. Sometimes never. Companies juggle quotes, invoices, and strangers. And the platforms in between take ten to twenty percent, and hold the money. TaskVault fixes both sides.

## 02-price — 01 · One project, three experts, each at their own price

A company splits a project into tasks, each with a budget. Freelancers apply with their own price, a message, and their skills. The company picks one per task, helped by a simple, explainable score. The price never enters the score.

## 03-sponsor — 02 · Sponsorship

New account, no history? A freelancer you already trust can sponsor them. One hop only: only the wallets you follow count, so a ring of fake accounts shows nothing. A hint, never a verdict.

## 04-lock — Lock: the money moves first

Once a freelancer is chosen, the money moves first. Two signatures in Freighter, and the task's USDC is vaulted in a Trustless Work escrow on Soroban. Not with the freelancer. Not with us. Locked before the first line of code.

## 05-release — Deliver, validate, release

The freelancer delivers a link, and a fingerprint of the work goes on chain. The company validates and rates the work, one to five, then signs approve and release. Five seconds later, the freelancer is paid. No wire. No thirty days.

## 06-security — 03 · Nobody gets scammed, on either side

Nobody gets scammed, on either side. A seven-day review window: silence pays nobody. Disputes are settled on chain by an arbiter who can split the money. And a publication deposit: a ghost listing forfeits it to a public guarantee fund. We hold no key to your pay.

## 07-stack — 04 · Track Stellar Composed

Built on Stellar. Freighter is the identity and the signature. USDC settles in five seconds, for under a cent. Trustless Work escrows run on Soroban. Every step signed by you, public on stellar.expert.

## 08-close — Close

TaskVault. You name your price. The money is vaulted. The work gets paid. Built solo for Hack Meridian twenty twenty-six.
