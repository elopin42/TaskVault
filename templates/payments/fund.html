How it works

A company locks a publication deposit in a Trustless Work escrow before its project takes applications. It gets it back when the project is closed, or when nobody applies. If candidates are left waiting without anyone being chosen, or the project is cancelled after receiving applications, the deposit is sent here, on chain, by the platform.
