One project. Everyone takes the part they want.

A company posts a project cut into fixed-price tasks. Freelancers apply to the part they want with a message and their skills: the price is set by the company, nobody quotes one. The one chosen for each task is paid in {{ asset_code }}{% if escrow == "escrow" %}, from an escrow the company funds before the delivery{% else %}, straight from the company's wallet at validation{% endif %}.

  1. BLOCK 01COMPANY

    Split

    One project, cut into fixed-price tasks. Any freelancer can apply to any open task.

    off-chain
  2. BLOCK 02FREELANCERS · COMPANY

    Apply

    Freelancers apply with a message and their skills, no price. A score out of 100 (paid history and ratings with this company, matching skills) is a hint; the company chooses one per task.

    off-chain
  3. {% if escrow == "escrow" %}
    BLOCK 03COMPANY · SIGNATURE ×2

    Lock

    Once a freelancer is chosen, the company deploys and funds one escrow for that task. Delivery opens only when the money is there, and the freelancer can see it.

    deploy · fund {% else %}
    BLOCK 03FREELANCER

    Work

    The chosen freelancer does the work at the fixed price. In direct mode no escrow is deployed: nothing moves on chain yet.

    off-chain {% endif %}
  4. {% if escrow == "escrow" %}
    BLOCK 04SIGNATURE ×3

    Deliver, release

    The freelancer signs the delivery link into the escrow, the company validates, rates it 1 to 5, then signs approve and release. No invoice, no chasing.

    deliver · approve · release {% else %}
    BLOCK 04COMPANY · SIGNATURE ×1

    Deliver, pay

    The freelancer posts the delivery link, the company validates, rates it 1 to 5 and signs one {{ asset_code }} payment. No invoice, no chasing.

    payment {% endif %}
